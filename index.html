1. Introduction

Climb Your Day ("the App") is a habit-tracking game that helps you build daily habits by climbing a virtual mountain as you level up. Your privacy matters to us. The App works fully offline, and we do not collect, transmit, or share your personal data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no accounts or sign-in, has no servers, and does not connect to the internet.

3. Data stored on your device

To work, the App stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • Habits you create or select (names, icons, categories, schedules)
  • Daily completion logs and history
  • Progress data (XP, level, streaks, achievements)
  • App settings (theme, reminder preferences)

This data stays on your device. We cannot access it, and it is never sent to us or anyone else.

4. Internet access and third parties

  • The App does not require or use an internet connection.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties.

5. Notifications

If you enable habit reminders, the App uses local notifications scheduled on your device. These are not sent through any external server or push service.

6. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: to show habit reminders you set (optional).
  • File access (only when you choose): to export or import a backup file of your data.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other personal data.

7. Data export and backup

You can export your data to a JSON file on your device and import it later. Where you save or share that file is your choice, and it is your responsibility to keep it secure. We never receive this file.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by:

  • Deleting individual habits within the App
  • Using Settings → Reset Data
  • Uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data

Device-level backups (such as Google or iCloud backups) are managed by your operating system settings, not by the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region). Since no data is collected or transmitted, the App is suitable for all ages.

10. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device's protections (screen lock, encryption, OS updates). We recommend keeping your device secured.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example by adding features that affect privacy, we will update the effective date above and notify users through an App update. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
